About PetAdoption

We're dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming pets in need. Every pet deserves a second chance at happiness.

Our Mission

PetAdoption was founded in 2010 with a simple mission: to save the lives of abandoned and neglected pets. We work tirelessly to provide medical care, training, and love to every pet that comes through our doors, preparing them for their forever homes.
